0.11.0

November 22, 2026 Latest
  • Editable tempo track. Review automatically detected sections, then click the BPM and meter badge to edit a section. Changes, grid offsets and hi-hat fills are saved with the song.
  • Tap Tempo in a loop. Each press of Space plays a click. After two missed beats, the metronome continues in the tapped tempo. Apply updates the selected passage.
  • Pickups and playback. Count-In checks the first full bar against later bars. Home moves to the first count-in click, and Play reuses the prepared audio when possible.
  • Separate click sounds. Count-in and whole-song metronome have independent sounds and levels. The defaults are sticks for count-in and closed hi-hat for the metronome, both at 0 dB.
  • Manual control when detection is uncertain. If a reliable rhythm cannot be found, the audio still opens and the BPM and first downbeat can be set manually.

0.10.0

November 19, 2026
  • Loop and speed trainer. Select bars and loop them; the trainer raises the speed by 5% every two passes up to 100%. Speed now goes down to 50%.
  • Key. The app detects the song's key and shows it in the header. The new Key control shifts the song by up to ±6 semitones and keeps the tempo.
  • Solo and +6 dB. Every part has a solo button, and its slider now goes up to +6 dB. The click is its own part with mute and solo.
  • Markers and bars. Bar numbers on the ruler, Go to Bar, named section markers (M, keys 1–9). Markers also go into tempo.mid.
  • Song memory and undo. Tempo, the 1, pauses, levels, speed, click and loop come back when a song reopens. Edits to tempo, the 1, pauses and markers can be undone.
  • Set list. Open several songs and switch with N and P or a page-turner pedal.
  • Stage mix and even loudness. A backing track with the click alone on the left channel, click.wav in the multitrack export, and an option to save at −14 LUFS.
  • Faster and smoother. Seeking is instant, slowed playback no longer freezes the window, and the song click turns on and off during playback without a pause.
  • New header. The song, its key and file, and the output volume with the file buttons sit in three cards. Recent files appear on the start screen.
  • Windows. Separation runs on the graphics card with the most memory, and falls back to the processor if the card fails mid-song.
  • Help → What's New shows this list, and it opens once after an update.

0.9.1

November 18, 2026
  • Click through the whole song. A checkbox in the Count-in card keeps the click going on every beat to the end. Pick its sound (metronome, sticks, cowbell, ping or hi-hat) and level.
  • New build-up. Eighths only over the second half of the last bar, each hit louder than the one before.
  • Playback to the end. The preview plays the whole song, and slowed playback is prepared about four times faster.
  • Clicks never clip, and the audio output follows the system device without a restart.
